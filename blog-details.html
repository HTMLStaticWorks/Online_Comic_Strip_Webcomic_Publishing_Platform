Designing Panels for Mobile Viewports

By Alex Rivera | Published June 25, 2026

When transitioning from printed comic pages to digital publishing platforms, the biggest challenge is formatting gutters and panels for vertical mobile scroll layouts.

Traditional comic book pages read in a Z-shaped pattern—left-to-right, then top-to-bottom. But on mobile viewports (which make up over 80% of webcomic readership), readers scroll vertically. A page that crams 6 panels together becomes unreadable on a 6-inch phone. Instead, creators should isolate panels vertically with generous gutter spacing, ensuring the reader focuses on one frame at a time.

Recommended spacing between consecutive panels.

Aim for a gutter space of 100px to 150px between narrative actions. This creates suspense, control pacing, and guarantees speech bubbles don't overlap awkwardly with surrounding art frames.
