Behind the Panels

Discover my creative journey as a comic strip artist, web developer, and founder of ComicVerse.

Hi, I'm Alex Rivera

I've spent the past 8 years sketching, writing, and formatting webcomic episodes for readers across the globe. After experiencing the limitations of mainstream publishers, I built ComicVerse as an independent portfolio platform to connect artists directly with fans who support original stories.

I handle all components of comic creation—from scripting character dialogues to ink vector coloring and publishing. I believe webcomics deserve clean, customizable homes, rather than congested social media feeds.

My Creative Roadmap

Key milestones in my independent publishing career.

2024: Launching ComicVerse Platform

Began coding the publishing suite to host multi-panel strips and monthly subscriber management channels.

2022: Published "Tales of Nebula" Book

Compiled and printed my first physical comic anthology book, funding it through reader pre-orders.

2018: Full-Time Freelance Illustrator

Transitioned into drawing original daily three-panel comic strips full-time, working with various publishers.

My Creative Philosophy

The principles that guide my illustrations and platform developments.

Creator Autonomy

I believe artists should retain 100% ownership and copyright of their storylines, layout files, and panels.

Direct Support

Pledging directly to creators builds healthier hubs, completely bypassing mainstream middlemen publishers.

Infinite Scalability

Vector-clean lines ensure comic panels look beautiful on phone screens, retina viewports, and paperback books.

My Digital Studio

I sketch all comic panels on a 24-inch high-resolution drawing screen tablet, using specialized vector-inking software. This setup allows me to clean outlines instantly and match color paletting gradients directly to the platform's requirements.
